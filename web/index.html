FAQ

Common questions about earning with WaitJI AI

How much can I realistically earn coding with WaitJI AI?▼
A single machine typically earns ₹2,000–4,000 a month from direct ad revenue share, that's the honest, industry-realistic number. Adding a referral network can bring combined earnings toward ₹9,000/month, depending on how many developers you invite. There's no guaranteed ceiling, use the calculator above for your own estimate.
How is this different from foreign ad-reward apps?▼
Most international platforms settle through Stripe, which can't pay out to most Indian developers, your balance shows up but never actually withdraws. WaitJI AI pays directly to UPI, with a ₹100 minimum withdrawal.
What's the minimum withdrawal and payout time?▼
Minimum ₹100. Requests are processed via Razorpay Payouts and typically land in your UPI account within 2–5 business days. No hidden fees.
Does this slow down or interrupt my coding?▼
No. The sponsored line only appears during Claude Code's own AI thinking pause, a window that already exists whether or not WaitJI AI is installed. There are no popups, no extra clicks required, and no change to your actual workflow.
Is my code or data ever read or sent anywhere?▼
No. WaitJI AI only detects whether Claude Code is actively in its thinking state, used purely to time when an ad displays. It never reads, stores, or transmits your code or file contents.
