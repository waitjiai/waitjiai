FAQ
Common questions.
Is ₹15,000/month realistic?▾
Honestly: not from one machine alone — that earns ₹2,000–4,000 (this is the industry reality; foreign platforms pay the same or less). ₹15,000 needs a referral network. Invite 30–50 developers and earn 10% of their earnings for life. Direct + referral + premium combined gets you there. We don't sell fantasies — check the calculator.
Will 3 ads be annoying?▾
No. Ads are a single line in the status bar and rotate based on how long the AI takes. A 3-second wait shows one; a 15-second wait shows three. Your flow never breaks.
How does fraud protection work?▾
Every click runs through velocity, cadence, IP and CTR checks. Bot-like or farmed clicks are blocked and never billed to advertisers — protecting their budget and keeping the marketplace honest. Advertisers see exactly how much fraud we saved them.
When and how do I get paid?▾
Withdraw from ₹100 straight to UPI — usually 2–3 business days. No bank-account hassle, no minimums beyond ₹100.
Is this legal? Does it break Claude's terms?▾
Yes, it's legal. It's a VS Code extension showing status-bar text — a standard documented use of the Extension API. No Claude terms are violated and we claim no Anthropic partnership. It runs only on your local machine.
