Questions

Before you start

Is there a minimum contract length?

No. You set a budget, it runs until spent or you pause it. There's no recurring commitment.

How am I billed?

Spotlight bills per 1,000 impressions; clicks on either placement are billed separately at a small multiple of the impression rate. You never spend past the budget you set.

Can I target by country or time of day?

Yes — campaigns can be scoped to specific countries (with local pricing) and to morning, afternoon, or evening IST windows.

What happens with invalid clicks?

Our fraud engine flags abnormal click velocity, IP abuse, and CTR anomalies automatically. Flagged activity is never billed to your account.
