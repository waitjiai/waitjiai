Culture

How we work

We're a founding team, not a corporate. Things are fast, blunt, and exciting.

We ship, then polish.

The codebase is clean but the timelines are aggressive. If you need 6 months of planning before writing a line of code, this is the wrong place. We push to production daily.

We argue about ideas, not egos.

If you have a better way to do something, say it directly. We don't have time for politics. The best idea wins, regardless of who it came from.

Builders first.

Every person on the team builds something. There are no pure managers here at this stage. The CTO writes backend code daily. That's the culture.

From Udaipur, for the world.
