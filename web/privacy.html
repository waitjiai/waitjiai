Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

WaitJI AI ("WaitJI AI", "we", "us", "our") is operated by QivaLabs LLP (LLPIN: ACV-6746), Udaipur, Rajasthan, India. This policy explains what data we collect across our VS Code extension, website, and dashboards, and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, and for advertisers, company name — collected via Supabase during sign-up.
  • Payout details: your UPI ID, used solely to send your earnings.
  • Usage data: ad impression and click events, timestamps, and IP address — used to calculate earnings and to run fraud detection (click-velocity, IP-rate, and CTR-anomaly checks).
  • Advertiser campaign data: ad text, destination URL, bid amount, and budget for campaigns you create.
What we never collect: the WaitJI AI extension only detects whether Claude Code is currently in its "thinking" state, purely to time when a sponsored line is shown. It never reads, stores, or transmits your source code, file contents, terminal output, or prompts.

2. How we use your data

  • To calculate and pay out developer earnings via UPI.
  • To detect and block fraudulent clicks and impressions, protecting advertiser budgets.
  • To run advertiser campaigns and provide campaign analytics.
  • To send account-related and, where you've opted in, product update emails.

4. Data retention

We retain account and earnings data for as long as your account is active, and as required to resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and meet legal obligations. You can request account deletion by emailing us (see Contact below).

5. Your rights

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the relevant address below. We'll respond within a reasonable time.

6. Security

Passwords are never stored by us in plain text — authentication is handled by Supabase. Payment card and bank details are handled directly by Razorpay and PayPal; we never see or store your full payment credentials.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date above.
