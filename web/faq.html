Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about earning UPI income with Claude Code and advertising to Indian developers.

👨‍💻 For developers
What is WaitJI AI and how does it work?
WaitJI AI is a VS Code extension that displays sponsored ads inside Claude Code's Thinking... spinner and terminal status line. Every time Claude Code processes a prompt, advertisers pay for that impression — and you keep 65% of the revenue, paid directly to your UPI ID. No workflow change, no extra work. Install once and let it run.
How do I install WaitJI AI?
Open Terminal and run:

curl -L https://waitjiai.in/vsix -o waitji.vsix && code --install-extension waitji.vsix

Or search WaitJI AI in the VS Code Extensions Marketplace. After installing, create a free account at waitjiai.in/login.html and paste your User ID into the extension settings.
How much can I realistically earn?
Earnings depend on how much you use Claude Code. A developer using Claude Code for 2 hours/day typically earns ₹480–660/month with both placements active. Heavy Claude Code users — those running automated coding sessions or using it for 4+ hours daily — can earn significantly more. There is no cap on impressions or earnings.
Does WaitJI AI slow down Claude Code or VS Code?
No. The extension runs a background polling daemon (poller.mjs) that fetches new ads every 90 seconds. This is a tiny HTTP request — less than 1KB. It does not intercept Claude Code's processing, does not add latency to AI responses, and does not read your code, prompts, or files.
What is the minimum payout and how fast do I receive it?
Minimum withdrawal is ₹100. After you request a withdrawal from your dashboard, funds arrive in your UPI ID within 2–3 business days. There is no maximum — you can withdraw your full balance at any time above ₹100.
Can I refer other developers and earn more?
Yes. WaitJI AI has a referral program that pays you 10% of your referrals' earnings — for life, with no cap. Share your referral link from the dashboard. When someone signs up using your link and starts earning, you automatically receive 10% of every impression they generate.
What if there are no active advertisers?
WaitJI AI runs house ads when no paid campaigns are active. This ensures the spinner always shows something (typically a WaitJI AI brand message) and keeps the extension active. You do not earn on house ad impressions — earnings only happen on paid campaign impressions.
Why don't my earnings always match my Claude Code activity?
Every impression and click is checked automatically by our fraud protection system — things like impressions arriving faster than 1/second, clicks faster than a human reaction time, or unusually high click-through rates. Activity flagged by these checks is not billed to advertisers and is not credited to your earnings, even though it still happened on your machine. This is what keeps advertisers paying for genuine attention, which is what keeps the marketplace funded. Normal Claude Code usage — even heavy daily use — is never affected; these checks are tuned to catch bot-like patterns, not real developers working normally. See fraud protection below for the exact thresholds.
📣 For advertisers
Who sees my ads on WaitJI AI?
Your ads reach active software developers who use Claude Code inside VS Code daily. These are typically professional engineers, startup founders, and freelance developers in India — a highly valuable, tech-savvy demographic. Most users are in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Pune.
What is the difference between Spotlight and Stream ads?
Spotlight (₹800/1K) appears inside the Claude Code animated Thinking... spinner — the moment where the developer is watching the screen and waiting. This is the highest-attention placement. Minimum budget ₹5,000.

Stream (₹300/1K) appears in the terminal status line while Claude Code is running. High volume, great for brand awareness campaigns. Minimum budget ₹2,000.

Both run independent auctions and can be run simultaneously.

• Indian state — all 15 major states including Maharashtra, Karnataka, Delhi, Tamil Nadu

Pricing is PPP-adjusted per country. Indian campaigns are priced in INR; international campaigns in local currency but charged in INR at live exchange rates.
How long does campaign review take?
After payment, campaigns are reviewed within 24 hours. Our team checks that ad text meets content guidelines (no misleading claims, no adult content, no competitor attacks). Most campaigns are approved within 4–8 hours during business hours (10 AM – 7 PM IST).
Can I get a GST invoice?
Yes. From your advertiser dashboard, click 🧾 Invoice on any campaign to download an invoice from QivaLabs LLP. If QivaLabs LLP is GST-registered at the time, the invoice includes a CGST (9%) + SGST (9%) breakdown; if not yet GST-registered, the invoice correctly shows "Not GST registered" and charges zero GST — we only add GST once registration is actually in place, as required by Indian law. You can provide your GSTIN during account setup for B2B invoicing.
What is the typical CTR on WaitJI AI?
Platform average CTR is 0.4–0.8% — significantly higher than traditional display advertising (typically 0.05%). This is because ads appear at a high-attention moment (while waiting for AI responses) to a technically sophisticated audience actively using a developer tool. Campaigns with clear CTAs and relevant messaging consistently perform above average.
⚙️ Technical
What fraud protection does WaitJI AI have?
WaitJI AI uses multiple layers of automated fraud protection: a cap of 10 clicks/minute and 60 impressions/minute per developer, a maximum of 50 clicks/hour per IP address, a 1.5-second minimum gap between clicks, and an automatic flag if your click-through rate exceeds 25% (normal CTR is under 2%). Invalid impressions and clicks are never billed to advertisers — and are never credited to developer earnings either. Advertisers only pay for genuine developer attention, and developers are only paid for genuine activity.
Which version of Claude Code do I need?
WaitJI AI works with Claude Code 1.x and 2.x. The extension uses the officially documented spinnerVerbs setting in ~/.claude/settings.json for the Spotlight placement, and statusLine for the Stream placement. Both settings are part of Claude Code's public configuration API.
Does the extension work on Windows, Mac, and Linux?
Yes. WaitJI AI works on all platforms where VS Code and Claude Code are supported — macOS (Intel and Apple Silicon), Windows 10/11, and Linux (Ubuntu, Debian, Arch). The polling daemon uses Node.js which is bundled with the extension.
Does it work with Cursor or other AI coding tools?
Currently WaitJI AI works specifically with Claude Code inside VS Code. Support for Cursor and Codex CLI is on our roadmap. If you use multiple AI coding tools, the extension earns whenever you actively use Claude Code — it does not interfere with other tools.
How does WaitJI AI protect my privacy?
WaitJI AI collects:
• Your User ID (to credit earnings)
• Impression timestamp and country (for ad billing)
• Click events (if you click an ad)

WaitJI AI never collects: your code, prompts, Claude Code responses, file names, terminal output, or browsing history. Bank account numbers are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Full privacy policy at waitjiai.in/privacy.html.
💳 Payments & billing
Which UPI apps work for payouts?
All major UPI apps work — PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, Amazon Pay, BHIM, and any UPI-enabled bank app. You can also add a bank account (IFSC + account number) for direct NEFT transfer instead of UPI. The sender name on your UPI receive notification will show as QivaLabs.
How do advertisers pay? Is Razorpay secure?
Advertisers pay via Razorpay — India's most widely used payment gateway, processing billions in transactions annually. Razorpay supports credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and wallets. WaitJI AI never stores your card details — all payments are processed directly through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.
What happens if my campaign budget runs out?
When a campaign's budget is 80% consumed, you receive an automatic email alert. When the budget is fully spent, the campaign pauses automatically. You can top up the budget from your advertiser dashboard and resume the campaign at any time. There is no penalty for campaigns that exhaust their budget.
Are there any taxes on developer earnings?
WaitJI AI pays out the full earned amount — we do not deduct TDS at source currently. However, income from WaitJI AI is taxable under Indian income tax law as income from other sources. Developers earning above ₹2.5 lakh/year should declare this income in their ITR. WaitJI AI provides a downloadable earnings statement from your dashboard for tax purposes.
